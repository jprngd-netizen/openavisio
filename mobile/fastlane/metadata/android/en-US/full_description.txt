OpenAvisio is a self-hosted vault for medical records and documents. This app is a thin companion client: it connects to an OpenAvisio instance that you or your care team runs, and gives you a native, biometric-locked way to reach it on Android.

The app stores no medical data itself. Everything lives on your instance — the app only remembers that instance's address and your app-lock preference.

This is a beta release. It is usable but still under active development, so expect rough edges.

Free software under the AGPL-3.0. No advertising, no analytics, no Google services, and no push-notification tie-in.
